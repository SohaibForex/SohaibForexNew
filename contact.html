FAQ

Common Questions

How do I install an EA?
After purchase, we provide full installation support via WhatsApp. We guide you step-by-step through MT4/MT5 setup, or do it remotely if you grant screen access.
Which brokers work with your EAs?
Our EAs work with any MT4/MT5 compatible broker. We recommend ECN/STP brokers like ICMarkets, Exness, or FBS for best results.
What is the minimum account size?
Most EAs can run on accounts starting from $100. However for optimal risk management, we recommend $500+ for most strategies.
Do you offer refunds?
We provide demos and backtesting results before purchase. Due to the digital nature of EAs, refunds are not available after delivery. Contact us before buying with any questions.
How do I receive EA updates?
All EA purchases include lifetime updates. When new versions are released, you'll be notified via WhatsApp group and the update will be sent directly to you.
Can I run EAs on multiple accounts?
Standard licenses are for one account. Multi-account licenses are available at a discounted rate. Contact us to discuss requirements.
